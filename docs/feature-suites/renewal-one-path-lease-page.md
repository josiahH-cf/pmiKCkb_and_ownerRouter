# S247 — Open a lease and work the current step

> Status: Guided orientation and current action delivered at `2e5e47a8`, intake 121. November 9 intakes 136 and 137 are AUTHORING READY for seven-step consolidation and the tenant-offer surface.
> November 12 intakes 159 and 161 are AUTHORING READY for in-place editors and an address that names the step shown (S281), and for showing only the steps that apply (S283).

**Goal.**

A staff member opens the right lease, sees which step needs attention and works it in place.
They can inspect another step and return to the current one without losing the meaning of either.

**Current state / intended end state.**

On the November 7 guarded replay of `ef81b891`, a prepared lease opened in Guided from
the desk table and from a Dashboard answer, both showing "Now: Ask the owner". Selecting
"Offer the tenant" by keyboard moved focus to that step. The stage and next step still
described the owner's message. The tenant action then read "Review and send", the same
wording seen in the prepared owner's step after it loaded. The step heading and recipient
preview therefore matter to distinguish what is being reviewed.

The covered fixture continued to the tenant offer. The assisted fixture led to program
contact. A lease with notice led to the non-renewal handoff. A later source end date offered
"Confirm this renewal is finished". A synthetic source read with a missing status kept
the prepared lease in the working batch and named the unavailable status. A fresh read
restored the status after the refresh window. The prior independent run walked the later
steps through explicit staff completion. That run's conditions and unresolved provider controls
retain their recorded scope. The intended page keeps these useful paths and makes the
current action, its target and its outcome clear with short, state-specific context.

The November 9 screenshots at the serving baseline show the same pricing and Status log
panels below multiple Guided steps, repeated prerequisites on the tenant offer, and
multiple versions of comparison and rent evidence. Check the numbers says “Numbers agree”
while a rent-charge anomaly needs action. The revision makes each step's decision and
material exceptions primary, with evidence and less common controls at named destinations.

**Actors and entry conditions.**

Existing authorized Renewal staff enter from a scoped desk row, a Dashboard answer, a
linked My Work item, an existing lease URL or a permitted process handoff. A source or
supporting read failure leaves the available lease page intact and labels the affected
part with its available recovery. Every authorized lease remains workable under S154.
The existing lease and operation guards determine who can view or act.

**What it is / how it functions.**

- **R-S247-1 — One path definition.** Use one ordered path: Check the numbers, Compare
  market rent, Ask the owner, Offer the tenant, Documents and signatures, Finish up
  and Complete. Each applicable step declares its existing control and handler,
  prepared facts, staff decision, waiting state, completion evidence and next step.
  Display the lease and unit, current cycle or review basis, and applicable notice or
  source uncertainty near the top so staff can recognize the record before acting.
- **R-S247-2 — Make the applicable path clear.** Show standing agreement coverage
  under S250 and the owner step again when a different rent requires it. Housing
  assistance follows S251's program contact, owner decision and rent sequence. The
  existing month-to-month review policy supplies its review date, eligibility and
  applicable work. Recorded non-renewal or move-out routes to its handoff. A later
  RentVine end date offers the existing confirm-or-keep-working choice. Completed,
  retired, closed, pending and unreadable states keep their own reasons. S255 owns
  retirement. Show the steps that apply, including an annual review whose next action
  is a comparison, and say on the path line why a standard step is absent. A step
  outside this lease's path is not shown (amended on November 12, 2026 by owner
  direction, S283 R-S283-7; the earlier text labelled such a step in place).
- **R-S247-3 — Keep one current recommendation.** Consume the shared evidence-based
  stage and next step owned by S248. On normal entry show the current step. An
  explicit task or a deliberate inspection opens that step with the current next
  step still identifiable and "Show current step" available. While following the
  current step, staff see it move after its evidence changes. Staff inspecting another
  step stay there until they choose to return. Desk, Dashboard, lease and My Work
  use the same lease/cycle and applicable recommendation. Supplemental lifecycle and
  action statuses stay available in Full and the desk table.
- **R-S247-4 — Put the work in the Now card.** Use one main landmark and one named Now
  card. Keep the shown step's actual fields and handlers in the card when that step
  owns them, including assisted owner and rent choices where applicable. Keep usable
  input when changing views or following a prerequisite. Apply R-S247-14 and
  R-S247-15 in both views. Put the primary action beside the inputs or consequential
  alert that governs it. Use one short outcome cue and an audience-specific action
  name. S249 and S224 supply preparation and exact review. The later steps use the
  existing document, source preview, follow-up and actual completion handlers. Name
  the destination and consequence of an external handoff and the next action on return.
- **R-S247-5 — Explain waiting and recovery in place.** Read S248 evidence to distinguish
  waiting on a person, scheduled, draft, delivery failure, ambiguous attempt, held
  input and confirmed completion. Show who needs to act, since when, the last known
  contact and what the app is watching when applicable. Offer the existing exact retry,
  record, review or reconcile action for its state. Show legitimate independent later work while waiting.
  Preserve meaningful warnings about permission, source currency and blocked inputs.
- **R-S247-6 — Keep the path advisory.** Staff may open or work an applicable step in
  any order. Existing required facts, role, operation review and confirmation govern
  its action. Path selection alone changes presentation and grants no effect.
- **R-S247-10 — Fit and focus the work.** At 1280 by 800 the header, path strip and
  primary action fit without scrolling on the first four Guided steps. At 390 pixels
  wide the primary action is within two viewport heights. On every Guided step and
  after landing at a Full section or named outcome within it, show the outcome and the
  primary action, exact route to the required input, blocker or waiting state in the
  first viewport from the outcome heading. Evidence starts closed. The named work
  control or its route comes before secondary controls and evidence in keyboard order.
  Test both themes, long program names, wrapping and visible keyboard focus. Selecting
  a step announces and focuses its heading, or the exact Work action when linked
  directly. See R-S247-15 for changes while staff stay on the same outcome.

- **R-S247-14 — One outcome and one owner in both views.** Use the same task definition
  and current lease/cycle state in Guided and the matching Full section (S258). Give
  each applicable outcome a heading and two distinct areas. **Work** shows the few
  values needed to decide, the current action and its required inputs or exact recovery.
  An uncommon action belongs to the same Work owner under a named secondary entry.
  **Verification** shows sources, freshness, calculations, prior decisions and receipts
  through a named read-only link or collapsed disclosure. Opening verification may use
  authorized reads. It creates zero saves, claims, paid lookups or dispatches. Put an
  explicit refresh, retry, edit, new operation preview or confirmation in Work. The exact
  review keeps its consequential inputs, targets and effect visible before confirmation.
  Use task-specific names for the action and the evidence link or disclosure.
  Optional history and rationale appear only where they explain the outcome.

  Assign each editable fact and business operation one owning outcome, shared across
  Guided and Full. Both views use the same saved and pending values, source and
  readiness projection, validation, handler, result and recovery for that owner.
  Render one actionable entry for it in the active view. A different outcome that
  needs the value shows the shared read-only summary and either a specific link to its
  existing owner, with exact return and retained input, or the same one editor revealed
  in place on the step shown, never two mounted at once (amended on November 12, 2026 by
  owner direction; S281 R-S281-11 and R-S281-14). Keep legitimate source, proposal,
  approved terms and receipt meanings separate. For example, Compare market rent owns
  Rent to recommend. Ask the owner shows that amount with Edit, which opens the same
  editor in place, while recording the actual owner's decision uses its own
  exact approved-terms input. A link, view switch or remount cannot re-submit an
  action or revive an older draft. S248 owns the single header status and note entry.
  Preserve existing independently useful actions and role guards by mapping them to
  their owning outcome. Remove repeated editors, competing shortcuts and generic
  Ready/You decide copy once their actual functions are accounted for.

  its existing focus and recovery rules when staff are following its result.
