# S74 — Tenant offer copy and channel truth

> Status: Active; waits on approved wording and evidence for channel claims.

**Goal.**

Let staff prepare accurate editable tenant offer wording without claiming a channel action that did not occur.

**What it is / how it functions.**

Copy comes from an approved versioned template, remains editable before draft creation, and records the actual draft/channel result separately from intent.

**Open questions & assumptions.**

Client must approve default wording, required fields, and when email/portal/channel statements are true.

**Cross-product impacts.**

Renewal workspace, approved templates, recipients, Gmail drafts, RentVine channel seam, and audit.

**Adversarial acceptance checks.**

- **AC-S74-1** — Rendered copy is traceable to an approved template version and exact lease facts.
- **AC-S74-2** — A channel status changes only from provider receipt/readback, never button intent.
- **AC-S74-3** — Owner and tenant recipients/content remain structurally separated.

**Forbidden actions / hard gates.**

No invented legal/offer wording, false sent/contacted status, direct send, or guessed portal action.

**Ordered prompt sequence.**

1. Approve wording and channel rules.
2. Build editable preview and truth states.
3. Verify provider result mapping without direct send.

**Deletion/merge recommendation.**

Keep until approved copy and channel-truth behavior are deployed.
