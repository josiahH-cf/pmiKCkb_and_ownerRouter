  2. **Choose the next action.** Open the lease (Focus view). Say what to do next and what it waits

     "← Back to renewals".
  3. **Check the facts.** In "Rent and charges" compare "Current rent", "Contractual lease rent",
     "Lease total (RentVine)" and "Unit listed rent (reference)" with "Working current rent"; save one
     permitted working edit, refresh and say what persisted and whether a source changed.
  4. **Prepare and schedule.** Review sender, every owner/tenant recipient, wording, attachments and
     Central time; choose "Schedule", fill "Schedule this reviewed communication" and choose
     "Schedule messages". Observe "Scheduled", then the attempt outcome ("Accepted by Gmail" or the
     exact unverified part).
  5. **Handle the reply and approval.** Observe the linked reply and the paused follow-up; record the
     owner's response under "Work recorded by staff" → "Owner response" with who, how and when;
     change one working term and see that the approval no longer covers it.
  6. **Prepare the documents.** In "Documents and completion" open the per-family readiness, choose
     the extension or full-lease context, review approved versions, mapped facts and each signer; a
     missing applicable fact blocks only that form and names its supplier.
  7. **Explain signing and return.** Follow "Open in Dotloop to send for signature" only when
     permitted; say who still must sign which version; show how a partial or wrong-version return is
     handled.
  8. **Review the updates.** Under the RentVine and Sheet update reviews read old/new/effective values
     and the terms basis; confirm only within the permitted environment; after readback say what
     changed, what still differs and the next step for anything partial or uncertain.
  9. **Return to the desk.** Reopen the same lease and cycle, explain its state and next action, and
     give a verdict and any remaining confusion in your own words.
