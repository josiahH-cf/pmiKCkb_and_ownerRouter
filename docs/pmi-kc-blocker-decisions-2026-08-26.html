How to use this

What this page does

  • Walks the blockers in the order they should be raised.
  • Records your decision, the reason, and who owns the next step.
  • Saves everything in this browser as you type.
  • Builds one Markdown block you can paste back to continue the work.

What it does not do

  • It runs nothing, deploys nothing, and sends nothing.
  • It never asks for a password, key, or token — every credential step here belongs to a person, not this page.
  • No network requests, no analytics. Your notes stay on this machine.
  • It records intent. Marking something “Decided” does not authorize it; a person still has to do it.

The statuses mean specific things. Decided — a choice was made and someone owns the next step. Deferred — deliberately not now; say when you will revisit. Blocked — cannot be decided here because someone or something outside this room is missing; name them. Not applicable — the premise no longer holds; say why.

Run details

Optional, but they make the copy-back far more useful later — a decision without a date and a room is hard to trust six weeks on.

Deliberately out of scope

  • Client names, addresses, and rent figures. None appear on this page.
  • Writing to RentVine or the operational spreadsheet. Both are closed and stay closed until a decision below opens them.

One fact worth leading with, because it will be asked. The app has 41 possible external actions defined. Six are switched on: read a mailbox, reply to a thread the user confirms payload-by-payload, apply a label, create an unsent renewal draft, create an unsent maintenance draft, and notify our own internal staff. Thirty-five are off.

So: the only thing the app sends by itself goes to us. Everything that reaches an owner or a resident is either a draft a person opens and sends, or a reply a person confirms. It writes nothing to RentVine and nothing to the spreadsheet. That is read from the committed action registry, not from a summary of it.

The decisions

Where the run stands

If a decision will not close

The blocker is a person who is not here

  • Mark it Blocked and name them in the blocker field.
  • Write the exact question you need answered, not the topic.
  • Agree a date to chase it. A blocker with no date returns unchanged.

The blocker is a vendor or a document

  • Mark it Blocked and name the vendor and the open thread.
  • Record what you would do if the answer never comes. Several items below already have a working fallback.

The room cannot agree

  • Mark it Deferred with a revisit date rather than forcing a decision that gets reversed.
  • Record both positions in the notes so the next session does not restart.

You learn the premise was wrong

  • Mark it Not applicable and say what changed.
  • This is a real outcome, not a cop-out. Three items in the last cycle were correctly refused this way.
