A practical 20-minute walkthrough

This route order demonstrates the product from intake through decision, execution boundary, attention, and administration without requiring a Live provider mutation.

1

Start at Console / and /ask

Ask a process question. A supported answer shows approved-source citations and freshness; an unsupported answer says no reliable source was found. Voice input, task capture, deterministic process detection, optional model-assisted classification, and explicit process start are available.

Expect: no invented answer, no automatic action, and an attention deck that links to decisions, connection work, and recent activity.

2

Open Spaces /spaces

Read the workspace directory, status pills, source/process context, and role-scoped destinations. Process definitions still exist but are surfaced beside their Space instead of as a primary navigation tab.

Expect: trusted publication/versioning and process deep links. Full self-service provisioning and the no-code page builder remain future suites.

3

Review Lease Renewal /lease-renewal

Inspect the lease desk, next-step signal, reconciliation conflicts, decision history, market-comp evidence, and proposal state. The persistent Test journey can move from Created through Done with durable non-Live receipts and zero provider construction.

Expect: Live source failures remain visible; Test never fills a missing Live projection. An unsent renewal draft is the final product-side initiation.

4

Use Approval Queue /approval-queue

Work the urgent decision inbox, open detail and history, approve, deny, return, snooze, assign, or use bounded bulk actions. High-risk and linked execution decisions require the correct role, exact context, and reason.

Expect: approving an app decision does not silently execute a provider effect. Consequential work remains separately confirmed and receipted.

5

Work Maintenance /maintenance

Capture or dictate an issue, validate the unit and blockers, create a ticket, filter the queue, update status, add notes, assign staff or the canonical Test Vendor, close, and reopen. Unverified intake is promoted or dismissed through an operator review boundary.

Expect: a Live ticket cannot use Test aliases or simulation, and a Test ticket cannot reach a Live provider.

6

Open Communications /gmail-hub

Review workflow-linked mailbox state, governed label rules, reply proposals, exact-confirmed replies, templates, and thread summaries. This is a workflow tool, not a general inbox or arbitrary compose surface.

Expect: no autonomous client send, no generic blast, no cross-mailbox Admin access, and no blind retry after an ambiguous provider result.

7

Check Notifications and Connections

Use the bell and notification center to open, clear, and tune internal attention. Connections shows read-only provider health to staff and bounded setup/verification controls to Admins where a real connector supports them.

Expect: an absent credential or endpoint stays unavailable. A connection card cannot turn green from browser input alone.

8

Finish in Admin /admin

Review users, roles, Space scopes, last-Admin protection, audit activity, reported issues, communications governance/retention, migration readiness, Vendor lifecycle, source configuration, and runtime controls.

Expect: exact confirmation and reason where authority changes; no secret, setup link, recipient body, or customer value in durable audit output.

Lease Renewal: from source facts to an unsent draft

Lease Renewal is the deepest internal workflow. It combines source-backed reads, reconciliation, human decisions, market evidence, approval, outreach preparation, and action-specific execution contracts.

Working workflow

Review and reconcile

Read Rentvine and Sheet projections when configured, classify leases, expose conflicts, resolve flags, and preserve property-linked decision history. Missing data is shown as missing, never replaced by Test values.

Evidence and suggestion

Market comps and rent guidance

Operators can capture comp evidence. The app may calculate a suggested rent, but that exact number enters a draft only after per-number Admin approval. The RentCast adapter remains inert without the approved plan/key.

Provider-specific

Draft, write, or document

Renewal initiation creates an addressed, unsent Gmail draft. A human sends from Gmail. Sheet, RentVine, Dotloop, portal, SMS, and Boom actions retain independent gates, contracts, and configuration.

Source-backed lease Review + decision Exact preview/approval Draft or gated provider effect
Source-complete action hardening The Sheet write-back and comp-screenshot paths now have immutable preview, one-attempt, receipt, reconcile, and rollback contracts on main. Their provider keys remain closed or blocked at their named external seams.

Maintenance: capture, route, work, and communicate

The current Maintenance desk is a persistent operator workspace with explicit Live/Test isolation and a separate external Vendor lane.

Operator desk

Ticket lifecycle

Validate capture, create a ticket, change status, add notes/activity, assign staff or Vendor, close, and reopen. Legal hold and product-record retention are preserved by the source-complete writers.

Draft-only outreach

Owner notice

The ticket resolves its property and authoritative owner contact, prepares an addressed Gmail draft, and stops. There is no in-app client-facing Send control; a person reviews and sends from Gmail.

Next workspace

Resident self-service

Guarded public intake primitives and quarantine review exist, while the complete resident-facing S47 experience, approved copy, and preferred RentVine channel remain a later authorized suite.

Provider boundary Photo storage, Live work-order creation/assignment/status, LeadSimple, and QuickBooks draft-bill actions do not become active from a working ticket. Each needs its documented provider contract, identity, mapping, receipt, and rollback.

Communications: workflow mail, not a second inbox

The Gmail surface is intentionally narrow. It operates on linked workflow threads and governed artifacts, preserving human send authority and bodyless durable evidence.

S51 hardening on main

Bad outcomes reach attention safely

Reply and watch failures/ambiguities now emit one value-free A2 event only after a durable terminal transition. Demo/Test emits no Live alert, replay does not duplicate it, and malformed environment configuration constructs no provider.

Permanent safety line No autonomous, scheduled, bulk, or model-triggered client-facing send. No generic new-message send, free-form mailbox browser, or cross-mailbox Admin access. Internal transactional notices may auto-send only to the locked managed staff destination.
Next hardening slice gmail.label.apply is already an enabled workflow action. The next source slice migrates it through the canonical S20/S25 one-attempt terminal contract without changing its gate or causing a second label mutation. The renewal and maintenance draft paths follow.
