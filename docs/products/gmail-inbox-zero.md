# Workflow Communications product lane

The filename is retained for compatibility. This is a workflow communication adapter, not a general
Gmail inbox.

## Current supported contract

The October 20 S229 amendment and November 3 serving release support workflow-linked renewal reads,
governed labels, exact linked replies, reviewed unsent-draft creation, and staff-confirmed
Send/Schedule/Resume through the existing composer. Source, deployed, observed and human evidence
retain the separate scopes in docs/facts.md and the current delivery evidence. Successful profile
or worker reads do not prove a customer dispatch.

S183 instruction authorizes human Send/Schedule only within its current amended exact renewal
contract. The managed sender, current record, recipients/thread, source values, rich/plain message,
files and current delivery-policy version bind each authorization. Changed content, sender or source
requires fresh review. Historical authorizations cannot dispatch under a superseded policy.

The two previously activated notice-send keys retain their recorded metadata; the maintenance
operation is retired before provider construction regardless of its historical open key.
Generic `gmail.message.send` remains closed. Both Dotloop write keys remain closed.
No autonomous client-facing send, generic or bulk send, model-triggered dispatch or unapproved
background send is available. Ordinary supported renewal staff use the existing exact confirmation,
not another generic client approval ceremony.

## Preparation, delivery and recovery

App-saved wording is distinct from a Gmail draft. Staff may review and exact-confirm an unsent
Gmail draft for manual sending, or review Send/Schedule in Communications when all current gates
pass. Resume repeats current review; copying or opening a composer grants no dispatch authority.

S189–S192 retain one durable at-most-once occurrence, current actor/Space/mailbox/source checks,
inbound-reply pauses, bounded worker visits and receipt/readback. Original draft, reply, dispatch,
failure and unknown-attempt history retain their actual meaning and own-attempt recovery.
Provider acceptance is not recipient inbox receipt. Ambiguity is reconciled, never blindly retried.

## Boundaries

- No free-form general inbox browsing, generic compose or blast send.
- Outside Gmail discovery/linking remains scoped; determine read side effects before refresh.
- Custom maintenance/intake/Vendor operational work and the resident-draft proof are retired;
  Vendoroo remains standalone. Retained records and original evidence are not purged.
- Sample/test content cannot create a real draft, send, schedule or production record.
- Missing sender/runtime/key, stale targets, conflicting edits and uncertain attempts hold only
  their named action. Connections, profile reads and visible buttons alone are not end-to-end proof.
- S182 Dotloop API-origin exclusions, managed identities, backend permissions and unrelated closed
  keys remain intact. Signing is a human Dotloop handoff.
- No customer send, draft, provider business write or production test record is verification proof.

Consult docs/loop-state.md and docs/evidence/application-delivery-2026-11-03.md for the serving
baseline; implementation changes are not deployed until their own release/readback evidence exists.
